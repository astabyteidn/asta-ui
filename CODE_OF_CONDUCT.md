# Code of Conduct

We want Astabyte UI to be a friendly place to work, for everyone. This code is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone. That holds regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that build an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient with others.
- Respect different opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Give useful feedback, and take it well.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to the people affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the whole community, not just for you.

## What we don't accept

- Sexual language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information, like a home or email address, without their clear permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be seen as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and any other project space. It also applies when someone officially represents the project in public, like posting from an official account or speaking at an event.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, email **hello@astabyte.com**. Every report is reviewed and investigated quickly and fairly.

Maintainers keep the reporter's privacy and safety in mind.

## What maintainers may do

Maintainers will clarify and enforce these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and will explain why when it makes sense.

Depending on how serious it is, they will take one of these steps:

| Step | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Correction** | Unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour | A private, written warning that explains what went wrong. A public apology may be asked for. |
| **Warning** | A single incident, or a series of them | A warning with consequences. No contact with the people involved for a set time. Further breaks may lead to a ban. |
| **Temporary ban** | A serious break, including ongoing inappropriate behaviour | A ban from any interaction with the community for a set time. |
| **Permanent ban** | A pattern of breaking the code, harassment, or aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people | A permanent ban from any interaction with the community. |
